Some exciting news, I have recently become part of the Visiting Academic Scheme with the UK Atomic Energy Authority (UKAEA) as a Senior Visiting Fellow. This allows for much greater collaboration between UKAEA to share our joint expertise and expand any future joint research opportunities. After working with UKAEA for a number of years already (including a co-funded student), I am really excited to continue to build this relationship and contribute to the fantastic work they are doing in the fusion energy area.
