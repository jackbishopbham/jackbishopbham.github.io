I recently gave a talk for the IReNA Online Seminar series (https://www.cenamweb.org/events/online-seminar-series) about mirror symmetry and boron-9. A recording of the talk will shortly be available on YouTube and a link provided here.

![IReNA Advert](/assets/img/IReNA.png)


It is really great to get a chance to present recent work by students in my research group to a broad audience where rather than focusing on the nitty-grity of the science - you get to practise presenting a scientific idea in a more basic way, really challenging yourself to make sure the work you are doing is actually interesting!
