The use of AI in many areas of science is taking off, but how about using using LLM to quickly generate outreach materials by giving it a recent paper published and getting a few minute overview at a suitable level for public dissemination.
I tried this last week with a recent paper by my student: A. D. Brooks et al. Phys. Rev. C 111, 024002 (2025) and passing it into the Google NotebookLM \url{notebooklm.google.com} where after ten minutes or so you can generate a video overview of whatever content you upload. See the results below:
<iframe width="1200" height="800" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/bCVT2yktNIU" title="Solving a 60 Year Mystery" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

While it is not perfect and is no substitution for a human-generated effort, it does a pretty good job at highlighting the key important factors of the paper and may generate some useful examples of how to explain this to non-specialists. The future is bright...
